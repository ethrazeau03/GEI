Our Story

Serving Stittsville &
Surrounding Communities

Gears Exteriors and Interlock is a locally operated business serving homeowners throughout Stittsville, Ottawa, Kanata, and the surrounding communities. We specialize in interlock installation, front patios, walkways, stone steps, concrete overlays, and a full range of exterior landscaping work.

We started with a simple goal: do great work, treat people right, and stand behind everything we build. That philosophy hasn't changed. Whether you're looking to completely redo your driveway, add stone steps to your front entrance, or freshen up a porch with a clean overlay, we approach every job with the same level of care.

Randy is the main point of contact for all our projects. Customers consistently describe him as prompt, professional, flexible, and easy to communicate with — qualities that matter just as much as the finished product. We believe the experience of working with us should be as good as the results we deliver.

With a 4.9-star rating across 81 Google reviews, we're proud of the reputation we've built in this community — and we work every day to keep earning it.
