Our Services

From interlock driveways to stone steps and porch overlays, we handle the full range of exterior and landscaping work that transforms the front of your home.

What We Offer

Exterior Work Done Right

Every service we offer is backed by the same commitment: quality materials, careful installation, and a finished product you'll be proud of every time you pull into your driveway.

Interlock Installation

Whether it's a brand-new driveway, a front patio, or a decorative courtyard feature, our interlock work is laid with precision. We plan the pattern, grade, and drainage properly from the start so your interlock stays looking great for years.

Front Patios

A well-designed front patio instantly transforms your home's curb appeal. We work with you on the layout and material choices to create an entrance that's both welcoming and built to last through every Ottawa winter.

Walkways

A clean, even walkway makes a difference in how your home looks and functions day-to-day. We install interlock and stone walkways that connect your home to the street, side yard, or garage in a way that's safe, durable, and sharp-looking.

Stone Steps

Stone steps add character and presence to any entrance. We source and install natural and manufactured stone steps that are properly set, level, and built to handle heavy use and freeze-thaw cycles without shifting.

Porch Overlays

If your porch is looking tired but structurally sound, an overlay can make it look completely new for a fraction of the replacement cost. We apply the overlay properly — with the right prep and finish — so it lasts.

Concrete Overlays

Concrete overlays are a smart, cost-effective way to renew surfaces that have seen better days. Whether it's a driveway apron, walkway, or patio slab, a quality overlay gives you a fresh, clean finish without the disruption of a full replacement.

Garden Borders

Clean, defined garden borders give your landscaping a polished, intentional look. We install interlock and stone borders that hold their shape, prevent grass from creeping in, and frame your garden beds beautifully year after year.

Laneway Borders

A border along your laneway adds structure, style, and a finished look to what's often one of the largest visible surfaces of your property. We lay laneway borders that stay straight and look great from the street.

Front Porch Repair

A crumbling or cracked porch is both an eyesore and a safety issue. We assess what needs fixing, give you an honest recommendation, and complete repairs that are built to hold up to our winters — not just look good for a season.

Exterior Landscaping

Sometimes a property just needs a proper cleanup and some thoughtful exterior work to come together. We offer general exterior landscaping to tidy up, define, and improve the overall look and functionality of your outdoor spaces.

Not Sure What You Need?

Give us a call or send us a message. We're happy to take a look and give you an honest recommendation — no pressure, no obligation.
